PDF and CDF: probability of an interval

P(x1 < X ≤ x2) = F(x2) − F(x1) Drag the ends of an interval along the x axis of the PDF or of the CDF, or along its probability axis: the probability appears as the area under the PDF and as a height on the CDF. X follows a normal, logistic, exponential or uniform distribution, with its parameters, PDF, CDF and quantile function; switch to the CDF, P(X ≤ x2), or zoom on x to see a narrow density.

Universality of the uniform

X = F−1(U) and F(X) ∼ Unif(0, 1) Quantiles projected through the quantile function of X, or through its CDF: the points gather where the density is high, or spread evenly over (0, 1). X follows a normal, logistic, exponential or uniform distribution, with its PDF, CDF and quantile function. A switch goes from one direction to the other, mirroring the curve and swapping the two densities; choose the quantiles, the mode and the unit.
