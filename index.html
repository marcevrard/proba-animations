Probability: interactive animations

Play, pause, or drag the slider; each animation has its own options.

PDF and CDF

F(x) = P(X ≤ x) Drag x along the axis of the PDF or of the CDF, or a probability along the CDF: the CDF at x is the area under the PDF up to x. X follows a normal, logistic, exponential or uniform distribution, with its parameters, PDF, CDF and quantile function. Switch to an interval, P(x1 < X ≤ x2) = F(x2) − F(x1), or zoom on x to see a narrow density.

Universality of the uniform

X = F−1(U) and F(X) ∼ Unif(0, 1) Quantiles projected through the quantile function of X, or through its CDF: the points gather where the density is high, or spread evenly over (0, 1). X follows a normal, logistic, exponential or uniform distribution, with its PDF, CDF and quantile function. A switch goes from one direction to the other, mirroring the curve and swapping the two densities; choose the quantiles, the mode and the unit.

Memoryless property

P(X ≥ s + t | X ≥ s) = P(X ≥ t) After waiting s, the remaining time keeps the law of X ∼ Exp(λ): its density, the tail beyond s moved back to 0 and scaled up, is the density of X again. Press play to let s grow, or drag s and t. Show the survival functions, S(x) = P(X ≥ x), instead of the densities: probabilities become heights, and the curve of the remaining time lies on that of X. Switch to Weibull(λ, γ) to see wear (γ > 1, less time left) or the survival of the fittest (γ < 1, more time left), and show the hazard rate: flat for Exp, which does not age, rising with wear.

Memoryless property: raindrops

Do the tiles still dry get their drop sooner? Rain starts on a pavement, and each tile gets drops at rate λ: its wait for the first one follows Exp(λ). Move s, the time already waited: fewer tiles are still dry, but their remaining wait keeps the same law. A table compares them with all tiles: the same share gets its drop within a second, with the same mean wait, 1/λ. Three views show it: the histograms of the waits, the share still dry t later (the curves lie on each other), and the mean wait against s, flat, where a drop that was due would come sooner and sooner. With fewer tiles (from 100,000 down to 4), the noise shows. Press play to watch the shower, or draw a new one.
