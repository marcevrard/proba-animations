Capture–recapture: how many bugs are left?

Two testers, one program: N̂ = mn/K Alice finds m bugs, Bob n, K of them in common: the overlap estimates how many bugs the program has, and how many are still hidden. Since Bob cannot find a bug twice, K is hypergeometric; the binomial would let him. Grow the number of bugs and watch HGeom(N, m, n) merge into Bin(n, m/N). With easy and hard bugs, both testers find the easy ones, and the estimate falls short.
