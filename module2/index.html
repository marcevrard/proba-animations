Screening for a rare disease

P(sick | positive test) ≠ P(positive test | sick) Set the population size, the prior (how rare the disease is), and the test's sensitivity and specificity. A tree of natural frequencies and the confusion matrix show who tests positive: with a 1% prior and a 95% test, only 95 of 590 positives are sick (16%). A curve shows how P(sick | positive) collapses as the disease gets rarer. Draw a random population, or retest the positives to update the prior with Bayes' theorem.
