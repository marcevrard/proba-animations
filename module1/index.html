The birthday problem

P(at least two of n people share a birthday) A class fills a calendar, person by person: shared days turn orange. Beside it, P(A) = 1 − 365·364·…·(365 − n + 1)/365n against n, an even chance from 23 people, with the share of 10,000 simulated classes. The n(n − 1)/2 pairs explain why it grows so fast, unlike the chance that someone shares my birthday.
